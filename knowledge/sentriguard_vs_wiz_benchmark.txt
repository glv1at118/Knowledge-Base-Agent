This internal note compares SentriGuard Cloud, Vantavo's upcoming cloud security posture management (CSPM) product, against Wiz, the current market-leading CSPM platform, to help the security product team understand where we are positioned well and where meaningful gaps remain before general availability.

SentriGuard Cloud's clearest differentiator is native, one-click automated remediation built directly into the core product. Wiz is primarily a detection and visibility platform; closing a finding usually means routing it to a separate SOAR or ticketing tool, which adds delay and an extra integration step for the customer. Our usage-based pricing, billed per monitored resource, also undercuts Wiz's typical enterprise contract minimums, which should make it easier for smaller, compliance-driven customers to get started. Regulated-industry buyers who are priced out of Wiz's usual enterprise deals, and who mainly need fast SOC 2 or HIPAA audit evidence, are the segment SentriGuard Cloud is best positioned to win first.

Wiz has been in market for several years longer, and it shows. Its agentless scanning covers a noticeably broader range of cloud providers and services, its partner and integration ecosystem is larger, and its customer base gives it a level of real-world battle-testing and brand trust that a pre-release product cannot yet claim. Any competitive pitch that leans on coverage breadth rather than remediation speed will lose to Wiz today.

In an informal internal test, scanning a 500-resource sandbox AWS account, SentriGuard Cloud surfaced initial misconfiguration findings in an average of 6 minutes, compared to roughly 4 minutes for Wiz in the same environment. Time-to-remediate told the opposite story: because Wiz findings still require manual routing to a separate tool, the same class of finding took roughly 45 minutes to close, versus about 3 minutes end-to-end with SentriGuard Cloud's native one-click fix. This was a small, single-environment, informal test, not a certified or statistically rigorous benchmark, and the numbers should not be quoted externally.

Based on this, the team's recommendation is to lead with remediation speed as SentriGuard Cloud's wedge into compliance-driven mid-market accounts, rather than competing on scan coverage breadth. Before pursuing larger, multi-cloud enterprise accounts where Wiz is entrenched, we would need to close the cloud-provider coverage gap and build out at least a small partner integration ecosystem.
