# SentriGuard Cloud Summary

**Product Category:** Enterprise Security

**Target Customer Industry:** Financial Services and Healthcare

**Description:**
SentriGuard Cloud is a cloud security posture management (CSPM) product by Vantavo. It is designed to continuously scan multi-cloud infrastructure for misconfigurations and exposed storage, offering one-click automated remediation. The product is particularly aimed at compliance-driven buyers, such as those needing to satisfy SOC 2, HIPAA, or PCI-DSS requirements, where automated audit-evidence collection is a key purchasing driver.

**Key Differentiators:**
- **Native One-Click Automated Remediation:** Unlike competitors such as Wiz, which focus primarily on detection and visibility and require separate SOAR or ticketing tools for remediation, SentriGuard Cloud includes remediation natively. This leads to significantly faster resolution of findings.
- **Usage-Based Pricing:** Billed per monitored cloud resource at $0.08/month, with volume discounts above 10,000 resources, making it accessible for smaller and compliance-focused customers.
- **Remediation Speed:** In comparative internal tests, SentriGuard Cloud achieved end-to-end remediation in about 3 minutes, versus 45 minutes for Wiz, despite Wiz offering slightly faster scan times.
- **Market Focus:** The key market entry strategy is to target compliance-driven mid-market accounts, using remediation speed as a primary differentiator.

**Strategic Recommendations:**
- Focus on mid-market, regulated buyers priced out of enterprise CSPM contracts.
- Lead with compliance and remediation speed in go-to-market messaging.
- Expand cloud-provider coverage and partner ecosystems if targeting large, multi-cloud enterprise accounts in the future.

**Advertisement Proposal:**
- Sponsor compliance-focused webinars with audit and legal partners.
- Publish a cloud misconfiguration risk report to attract leads.

**Product Portfolio Philosophy:**
Vantavo products, including SentriGuard Cloud, are intentionally focused and independently managed, aiming for simplicity over an all-in-one platform approach.
